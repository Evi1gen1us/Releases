Notea — Privacy policy

Notea keeps your notes on your phone. There are no accounts, ads or analytics, and nothing about you is sent to the developer.

Sync between devices (optional)

If you join your devices into a chain, or share a note with another person, Notea can pass changes through a sync server. Everything is encrypted on your phone with a key that exists only on your devices; the server stores only encrypted text it cannot read, and knows no names, emails or phone models. Photos and recordings are not sent to the server. Data nobody has used for nine months is deleted from the server automatically. Leaving the chain removes your device's mark from the server.

Google user data

  • What is accessed: only if you connect Google Drive (for backups or for syncing your own devices), Notea gets the drive.file permission. With it, Notea can create and read only the files it made itself. It cannot see your other Drive files.
  • How it is used: Notea keeps backup files of your notes in a “Notea” folder in your Drive (removing its own older backups beyond the newest ten) and, if you turned it on, encrypted sync files for your own devices in a subfolder of it.
  • Storage: this data stays only in your own Google Drive. Sync files are always encrypted on the phone; backups are encrypted if you set a backup password.
  • Sharing: Google user data is not shared with anyone, is not sent to the sync server or the developer, and is not used for ads or any other purpose.

Notea's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Stopping

Choose “Disconnect” for Google Drive in Notea, or remove Notea's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Files Notea made stay in your Drive until you delete them. To stop syncing, choose “Leave the chain”.
